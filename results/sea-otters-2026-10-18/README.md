# Sea otters in Alaska: staged replacement

This corrects the species selection. The installed 4.24-second preview is a
Eurasian river otter (Lutra lutra), and has not been relabeled or replaced yet.
The staged source is real sea otters (Enhydra lutris) at the ocean surface in
Unalaska/Dutch Harbor, Alaska, from NOAA's B-Roll: Sea Otters, video 2789953172001.

## Source and selection

[NOAA source and reuse terms](https://videos.fisheries.noaa.gov/detail/video/2789953172001/b-roll%3A-sea-otters)
identify this as B-roll and allow public-domain reuse with NOAA Fisheries credit.
The opening slate attributes all footage to NOAA; the Alaska segment slate names
sea otters and Unalaska/Dutch Harbor. Neither inspected slate lists an outside
rights holder. This is an asset-specific check, not an assumption that everything
on a government site has the same rights. The
[NPS species reference](https://www.nps.gov/olym/learn/nature/sea-otter.htm)
identifies sea otters as Enhydra lutris. The source's own identification and decoded
frames support the identification; being in the ocean alone would not distinguish
sea otters from river otters.

The selected source window is 122-142 seconds. Actual encoded duration is
19.986633 seconds: 599 frames at 30000/1001 fps, 854 x 480, silent, plays once.
Show "20 seconds" in ordinary rounded runtime text and retain 19.987 seconds in
precise technical evidence. It shows a raft floating on the ocean, followed by a
closer view. An original shot change remains near the end. No generated wildlife,
looping, speed alteration or cropping is used. Audio is removed intentionally.
Credit must read "NOAA Fisheries" and link the source on Details/credits when
activation is approved. Preserve the cinematic coastal menu design.
