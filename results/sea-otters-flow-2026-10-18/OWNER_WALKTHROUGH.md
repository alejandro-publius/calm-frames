# Owner walkthrough

Calm Frames is open in the existing Vega VirtualDevice. Start on **Home, Acadia selected, Watch focused**. No recording was made. These directions follow the observed installed layout; the owner was not presumed to have watched the live run.

## Direct route to Sea Otters

From **Home, Acadia selected, Watch focused**: **Up, Right, Select** opens
Sea Otters Details with Watch focused; **Select** starts playback. Thus the full
sequence is **Up, Right, Select, Select**. From playback, **Back, Back, Left, Down, Left**
restores Home Acadia Watch. This exact sequence was rechecked without rebuilding.

## A three-minute tour

1. Press **Up, Select**. The Acadia destination opens Details with Watch focused.
2. Press **Right, Select** for More info. Press **Back, Left, Select** to return to Watch and play Acadia.
3. Let the first play finish. In the current extra-cautious profile the replacement intervals are 7.343 to 12.699 seconds and 28.113 to 31.593 seconds. The native logs confirm requests for the baked ocean surface at both intervals; you still need to judge the actual transitions and original audio. Natural end returns to the launching screen.
4. From Details, press **Back** to the selected Acadia destination. Press **Right, Select** to open Sea Otters Details. **Right, Select** opens More info; **Back, Left, Select** starts the video. It is a 19.987-second silent NOAA sea-otter excerpt and plays once.
5. During playback, wait five seconds for controls to hide, then press **Down** to reveal them. **Play/Pause** pauses; the same key resumes. **Back** returns to Details Watch. **Back** again returns to the selected destination. Letting the sea-otter clip finish also returns to Details Watch.
6. From the Sea Otters destination, **Up, Select** opens Parents. Do not enter a PIN. **Back** returns focus to Parents. **Down** returns to Sea Otters; **Left, Down, Left** leaves Acadia selected with Watch focused.

## Acadia pause and Skip

From the starting point press **Select**. During the first replaced interval, **Play/Pause** requests the opaque paused fallback; the same key resumes the eligible ocean presentation. When the countdown's **Skip** control has focus, **Select** skips with protection requested before the playhead moves. **Back** returns to Home Watch. A separate uninterrupted play is the easiest way to see both replacements.

## How it works

From the starting point: **Up, Up, Right, Select** opens How it works (the intermediate header focus is the current profile). **Back** restores focus to How it works. The existing Check playback diagnostic is unavailable in the current extra-cautious profile because its legacy map covers WCAG only. No setting or trust was changed to expose it. The internal Harbor practice fixture remains tested but is not promoted to the two-destination consumer menu.

## What the hierarchy teaches

The large islands make the first decision spatial: which coast to visit. A real still and a short label identify each destination. Coral outline plus underline marks focus; the persistent lower edge marks selection after focus leaves. The lower Watch and Details actions belong to the selected destination. Details puts the heading and Watch on a cream panel, and loading keeps the same panel with static map art. Fraunces headings and Source Sans 3 controls retain the established typography. The map is original static SVG artwork, with no particles, flashes or animation.
