## Rules the take followed

- App-window capture of the Vega Virtual Device with app audio. No microphone,
  webcam, desktop or other window.
- Natural speed throughout. No speed changes, montage, flashing transitions, added
  music or voice. About one remote press every two to five seconds.
- No pause during playback: the virtual device records a black picture while
  paused.
- Only approved prepared titles were played. The import and recovery runs are shown
  as terminal cards rendered from their saved logs; their clips were never played.
- The approved line and no comfort, seizure or clinical claim.
