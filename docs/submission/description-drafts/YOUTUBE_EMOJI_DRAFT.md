🌊 Calm Frames gives a nature scene time to breathe.

Choose Ocean or Forest on a coastal TV map. Open the details, decide when to watch, and stay in control of when motion starts and stops.

McWay Falls provides five continuous minutes of a fixed coastal view at natural speed. The source audio fades at the end, then the final frame stays until you choose Back. No repeated loop or automatic return Home. Acadia, Sea otters and Forest open as still scenes, with short previews available separately.

🎮 THE APP FLOW

→ Browse Ocean and Forest with visible remote focus.
→ Read the title's duration and ending before playback.
→ Choose Watch explicitly.
→ Pause, resume or leave with Back.
→ Let McWay settle on its final picture.

The submission video is an excerpt of the app experience, not the entire five-minute scene. Final-frame behavior was checked separately in the native app.

🧠 THE CORE IDEA

Give the viewer time and control.
Keep the media preparation and verification traceable.

The app plays prepared editions with signed analysis maps. Build checks verify approved registrations and packaged files. Runtime checks bind the selected title and map before uncovering the picture, with separate cover scheduling and guarded seeks.
