## Rules that must survive your edits

- Ask only for feedback on wording and flow. No demo, no call, no data, no endorsement.
- Never offer to show anyone flashing content, and keep the line in each draft that says plainly that nothing we send contains flashing.
- Keep the approved sentence word for word: "reduces exposure to flashing above published limits in the videos it plays; not a medical device." Never "prevents seizures" or "seizure-proof".
- Stay truthful: a student hackathon project, early, not a medical device, and no measured results to quote. We have none yet, so quote none.
- Under 200 words each. Plain and warm. No jargon, no marketing.
- No em dashes or en dashes, and keep to the banned-word list under Style in CLAUDE.md.

## If someone replies

Say thank you and answer their question. Do not turn a reply into a second ask.

If you want to quote a reply in docs/VALIDATION.md (due Fri Oct 16), ask that person for permission first, in a separate message. The drafts deliberately do not ask for permission up front, because that would make it two asks instead of one.
