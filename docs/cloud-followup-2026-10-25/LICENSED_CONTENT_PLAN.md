## Smallest useful acquisition request, after source access is available

For each missing title, seek one original file with at least 300 continuous
seconds at natural speed, a stationary camera, and no editorial cuts, loops or
time stretching. Prefer clean nature ambience recorded with the picture; treat
music, voice and separately supplied audio as separate rights and review inputs.
Keep a silent option truthful. Duration alone does not establish comfort.

Collect the exact source page, creator, downloadable original, file SHA-256,
license version or written permission, required attribution, and a dated copy
of the relevant primary terms. Permission must cover local transformation,
redistribution inside the app and use in a demonstration, as applicable.
Check third-party footage, music and logos separately; a government-hosted page
does not establish ownership of every component. A subscription or streaming
download is not a grant to remove DRM or redistribute its content.

No purchase or broad rights acceptance is authorized in this pass. The specific
missing input is a qualifying file plus its source-specific rights record for
Forest and for Sea Otters. The existing short files cannot be made qualifying
by repeating them. If no qualifying rights-cleared source is available, retain
the current still-scene/preview labels and leave item 7 partial.
