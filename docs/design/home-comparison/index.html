What to look for

  1. In grayscale, can you find Play before reading anything?
  2. At large view, can you read the heading and controls from your usual viewing distance?
  3. Restore color. Does terracotta make the focused Play clearer without competing with the lighthouse?
  4. Review the selected A. Name one thing to keep and one thing to change; B remains a reference.
