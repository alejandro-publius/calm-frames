Check a clip

Drop in a video. This machine measures it against the WCAG 2.2 general and red flash thresholds and the broadcast rules, and hands back one self contained HTML report.

MAX_MB MB at most. It is analysed on this machine and deleted as soon as the report is written. It is never played, never shown and never sent anywhere.

What you get back

  • the flash timeline, drawn from the numbers and never from the picture;
  • every span that went over the thresholds, with its times;
  • what the player hides, on each of the three household presets;
  • the license line, if the file is one of ours, and the hashes.

The report never shows a frame of the clip. It describes a hazard, it does not display one. Nothing on this page plays the file you upload, before or after it is measured.
