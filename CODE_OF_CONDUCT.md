# Code of conduct

Calm Frames is built for families of children with photosensitive epilepsy.
People come to it worried about a child, or living with a condition of their
own. The way we treat each other here should make that easier, not harder.

## What we ask of everyone

- **Be kind and patient.** Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly.
- **Disagree about the work, not the person.** A review finding is about code or
  a claim, never about who wrote it.
- **Respect privacy and health.** Nobody owes anyone details about their own
  health or a child's. Do not ask for them, and do not pass on what someone
  told you.
- **Keep people safe.** Never post, link or attach flashing content, and never
  ask anyone to watch it. [docs/SAFETY.md](docs/SAFETY.md) applies to issues,
  pull requests and comments as much as to code.
- **Be truthful about what the project does.** Calm Frames reduces exposure to
  flashing above published limits in the videos it plays; not a medical device.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Mocking anyone's disability, health, identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting flashing or strobing media, or trying to get someone to look at it.
  That is a safety incident, not a disagreement.

This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, and anywhere
someone speaks for the project.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer, Alex, who is
[@alejandro-publius](https://github.com/alejandro-publius) on GitHub.

- If it can be described in public, open an issue.
- If it cannot, use the contact details on that GitHub profile, or open an
  issue that says only that you need a private way to reach the maintainer,
  with no details in it, and you will be given one.
- If someone has posted flashing content, do not repost it or link to it. Say
  where it is (the issue or pull request number) and nothing more.

The maintainer aims to read every report, to keep the reporter's name private,
and to say what was done.

## What the maintainer may do

Edit or remove comments, close or lock a thread, and block someone from the
repository. Flashing content is removed first and discussed afterwards.
